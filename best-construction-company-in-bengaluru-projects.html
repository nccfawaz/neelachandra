How to Choose a Construction Company in Bengaluru

When comparing construction companies in Bengaluru, verify these before you commit:

1. Verifiable completed projects. Ask for named, visitable projects, not renders. Neelachandra has delivered for Honda Cars India, Mandot Steel, VRL Automation and Recipharma.

2. In-house design and structural engineering. A single team from design to handover prevents coordination gaps and delays.

3. Sector experience that matches your project. Industrial, KIADB, villa and commercial builds each need specific expertise.

4. Documented quality and timeline process. Look for material traceability, site supervision and milestone-based delivery, the same standards Neelachandra uses for OEM-grade clients.

5. Transparent pricing. Insist on itemised, stage-wise costing with no hidden extras.
