Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Neelachandra Construction ("Company", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our website:

a. Information You Provide Directly

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you fill out a contact form
  • Project details or messages you submit through inquiry forms
  • Email address if you sign up for our newsletter or marketing updates

b. Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected through cookies and analytics tools
  • General location data based on IP address

c. Information from Third-Party Tools

  • Interaction data from tools such as Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook) Pixel, and WhatsApp click-to-chat, as described in Section 3 below

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested information about our services
  • Contact you regarding project consultations or quotations
  • Send newsletters, updates, or marketing communications, where you have opted in
  • Analyze website traffic and user behavior to improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Tools and Cookies

We use the following third-party tools on our website, which may collect data about your visit:

  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand website traffic and visitor behavior. Google may use this data in accordance with its own privacy policy.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel: Used to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to show relevant ads to visitors on Facebook and Instagram.
  • WhatsApp Click-to-Chat: If you contact us via WhatsApp through our website, your conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms.

These third parties may use cookies and similar tracking technologies. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect certain website features.

4. Newsletter and Marketing Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter or marketing updates, we will use your email address to send you relevant updates about our projects and services. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link provided in our emails or by contacting us directly.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Third-party service providers who help us operate our website and marketing tools (e.g., Google, Meta, WhatsApp), subject to their own privacy policies
  • Legal authorities, if required by law or to protect our rights

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including responding to inquiries, maintaining business records, and complying with legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 10.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of this website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
