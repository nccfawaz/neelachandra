How Neelachandra Builds Your Home in Bengaluru From First Drawing to Key Handover

Our Process

Every Neelachandra project follows a transparent, milestone-driven process so you always know what stage your build is at, what has been spent and what comes next. No surprises, no ambiguity.

Consult & Plan

Tell us your plot location, approximate area and vision. We assign a dedicated Project Coordinator, conduct a site visit and soil test, and prepare initial architectural drawings before you commit to anything.

Design & Approve

Review your 2D floor plans, 3D elevations and structural drawings. We finalise the full scope, material specifications, timeline and total cost in a signed agreement. Price locked. No changes without your written approval.

Build & Update

Construction begins with branded, BIS-certified materials fully documented at every stage. Your Project Coordinator sends weekly progress reports with site photos, and our 24x7 live site access lets you check in remotely whenever you want.

Handover & Move In

We conduct a full quality walkthrough before handover. You sign off only when satisfied. Keys, warranty documents, compliance certificates and as-built drawings are all handed over on the same day.
