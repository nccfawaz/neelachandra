Frequently Asked Questions Regarding Home Construction Packages in Bengaluru

Q: How much does it cost to build a house in Bengaluru in 2026?

A: Our construction costs range from ₹2,299 per sq ft (Silver) to ₹3,499 per sq ft (Diamond). For a standard 1,200 sq ft home, this translates to roughly ₹27 lakhs to ₹42 lakhs. These are fully turnkey rates covering design, structure, basic finishes, plumbing, and electricals.

Q: How much does it cost to build a house on a 30×40 site in Bengaluru?

A: A 30×40 plot (1,200 sq ft) with a G+1 structure typically yields 1,800–2,000 sq ft of built-up area. Using our popular Gold package at ₹3,099 per sq ft, the total cost ranges from ₹55 lakhs to ₹62 lakhs — complete with premium finishes and smart home automation.

Q: What is included in your turnkey construction packages?

A: Every package includes structural and architectural design, soil testing, excavation, RCC framework (using branded steel and cement), masonry, internal and external plastering, flooring, painting, electrical wiring, plumbing, waterproofing, an underground sump, and an overhead tank.

Q: Are there hidden costs? What is excluded from the per sq ft rate?

A: We state all exclusions upfront in your detailed estimate. Items outside the base rate include: government statutory fees (BBMP/BMRDA), temporary BESCOM/BWSSB meter deposits, borewell drilling, external compound walls, and hard rock-breaking if required during excavation.

Q: What if cement or steel prices rise during construction?

A: Once your agreement is signed, your per sq ft price is completely locked. Neelachandra absorbs all market fluctuations on the branded materials specified in your contract. You will not face any cost escalation unless you formally request a scope change or material upgrade.

Q: How is the payment schedule structured?

A: We follow a milestone-based payment schedule across 10–12 stages tied directly to physical site progress — footing completion, plinth level, individual slab castings, plastering, and so on. You never pay a large lump sum upfront.

Q: Are BBMP or BMRDA plan approval fees included in the package?

A: We handle the complete liaison, documentation, and architectural drawings required for BBMP, BMRDA, or Gram Panchayat plan sanctions. The actual statutory fees, taxes, and government deposits are paid directly by the property owner as required by law.

Q: Is a soil test mandatory before building in Bengaluru?

A: Yes — and it is included in all our packages. Bengaluru's soil varies drastically from hard rock to loose earth. Testing lets our structural engineers design the correct footing depth and foundation type, ensuring your home is structurally sound.

Q: Do you assist with permanent BESCOM and BWSSB connections?

A: Yes. We prepare the technical documentation, building plans, and structural completion certificates required to apply for your permanent BESCOM electricity allocation and BWSSB water and sewerage lines.

Q: Do packages include rainwater harvesting and underground sumps?

A: Yes. All Neelachandra residential packages include an underground water sump sized for your built-up area, a branded overhead tank, and a complete rainwater harvesting system compliant with BBMP regulations.

Q: What brands of cement and steel do you use?

A: We use exclusively Tier-1 materials. For cement: UltraTech, ACC, or Birla Super (OPC 53 Grade for structural works). For TMT steel: JSW Neo, Tata Tiscon, or Indus (Fe500D or Fe550D) — specified per package and stated in your agreement.

Q: How do you ensure concrete strength and quality on site?

A: We run slump tests before every concrete pour and cast cubes for 7-day and 28-day compressive strength testing at certified third-party laboratories. You receive test reports as part of your project documentation.

Q: Can I upgrade specific materials like flooring or bathroom fittings?

A: Absolutely. Our packages are fully flexible. If you choose the Silver package but want Italian marble or premium Kohler fittings, you simply pay the differential between the package allowance and your chosen upgrade.

Q: What warranty do you provide after handover?

A: Every Neelachandra home comes with a 10-year structural warranty covering foundations, columns, beams, and slabs. We also provide a 1-year warranty on waterproofing integrity and general plumbing and electrical fixtures.

Q: How long does it take to build a G+1 or G+2 house in Bengaluru?

A: A standard G+1 or G+2 home takes 10 to 14 months from excavation to final handover. We attach a binding timeline with sub-milestones to your agreement so you always know exactly where your project stands.

Q: Will there be a dedicated site engineer on my project?

A: Yes. A dedicated, qualified site engineer is assigned to your project. They oversee daily labor, ensure adherence to structural drawings, coordinate material delivery, and perform routine quality checks — so you do not need to visit the site daily.

Q: Do your rates apply across all of Bengaluru?

A: Yes. Our package rates apply uniformly across Bengaluru — including North Bengaluru, Tumkur Road, Doddaballapura, Whitefield, and Sarjapur. Site-specific factors like narrow road access are assessed during our free site visit and quoted transparently before you commit.
