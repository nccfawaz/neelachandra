Common Questions About Our Construction Services in Bengaluru

Q: Can I hire Neelachandra only for architectural design without a construction contract?

A: Yes, Neelachandra offers standalone architectural and structural design services across Bengaluru. You can hire our team exclusively to create floor plans, 3D elevations, structural blueprints, and electrical/plumbing layouts, even if you plan to use an independent contractor for the physical construction.

Q: What drawings are included in a standalone architectural design package?

A: Our standalone design package provides a comprehensive, execution-ready blueprint set. This includes 2D floor plans, 3D exterior elevations, structural column and footing layouts, detailed center-line drawings, electrical routing charts, plumbing line designs, and municipal approval drawings optimized for BBMP or BMRDA sanctions.

Q: Do your independent architectural services include structural engineering certifications?

A: Yes. Every standalone architectural project includes comprehensive structural calculations and load-bearing designs vetted by certified structural engineers. We provide the structural stability certifications required by local authorities and financial institutions for home loan approvals in Bengaluru.

Q: What types of property renovation services do you offer in Bengaluru?

A: Neelachandra provides turnkey residential and commercial renovation services. This includes structural modifications, building extensions (adding extra floors), full interior remodeling, modular kitchen upgrades, bathroom waterproofing overhauls, exterior painting, and structural strengthening for older independent houses.

Q: Can Neelachandra renovate an old independent house to add a new floor?

A: Yes. Before adding a new floor to an existing building, our engineering team conducts a thorough structural health assessment and non-destructive testing (NDT) to check foundation strength. If the existing structure allows, we manage the complete process, including structural reinforcement, architectural layout matching, and execution.

Q: How do you estimate the cost of a house renovation project?

A: Renovation costs are calculated based on a detailed bill of quantities (BOQ) rather than a flat per-square-foot rate. We conduct a free site inspection to assess the current structural condition, map your exact requirements, and provide a transparent, itemized quote detailing material costs and labor charges.

Q: Do I need BBMP plan approval for structural renovations or extensions?

A: Internal modifications and minor repairs do not require formal approvals. However, structural extensions — such as adding a first or second floor to an existing building — require revised building plan sanctions from the BBMP, BMRDA, or relevant local panchayat. Our team assists in managing this entire documentation process.

Q: What waterproofing services do you provide for existing buildings in Bengaluru?

A: We specialize in advanced, multi-layer waterproofing systems for existing structures. Our services include terrace waterproofing (using polyurethane or crystalline coatings), bathroom tile grouting, external wall dampness treatment, overhead water tank sealing, and heavy-duty basement waterproofing to resist high water tables.

Q: How long does terrace waterproofing take for a standard house in Bengaluru?

A: A standard residential terrace waterproofing project (covering 1,000 to 1,500 sq ft) typically takes 3 to 5 working days. This includes thorough surface preparation (jet washing and crack filling), chemical application coats, and required curing times to ensure a completely seamless, leak-proof barrier.

Q: Which waterproofing chemical brands do you use for structural repairs?

A: We use strictly premium, industry-standard chemical systems from market-leading brands, including Fosroc, Dr. Fixit, Sika, and Asian Paints SmartCare. The specific compound (epoxy, acrylic, or polyurethane) is selected based on the severity of the leakage and the surface type.

Q: Do you provide a warranty on your leak-proofing and dampness treatments?

A: Yes. All professional waterproofing and structural repair works executed by Neelachandra are backed by a documented application warranty ranging from 5 to 10 years, depending on the chosen system. This warranty covers both material performance and application integrity.

Q: Can third-party contractors rent construction machinery from Neelachandra?

A: Yes. Our commercial equipment rental fleet is fully available to independent contractors, developers, and individual home builders across Bangalore. You can lease our machinery regardless of whether Neelachandra is managing the primary construction project.

Q: What types of construction equipment do you provide on rent in Bengaluru?

A: Our rental fleet includes concrete mixers, transit mixers, earthmovers (JCBs), centering and shuttering materials (MS plates and props), structural scaffolding, concrete vibrators, and heavy-duty dewatering pumps for deep excavations.

Q: Which areas in Bengaluru do your equipment rental services cover?

A: Our logistics network delivers construction equipment directly to sites across Bangalore Urban and Rural, including Nelamangala, Doddaballapura, Whitefield, Sarjapur, Electronic City, and the entire Tumkur Road industrial corridor.

Q: How are your equipment rental charges structured — daily vs. monthly?

A: We offer flexible rental structures customized to your project timeline. Machinery can be leased on a daily hire basis for short-term tasks or via cost-effective monthly contracts for long-term project lifecycles. Transportation and operator costs are detailed transparently in the rental agreement.

Q: How do you ensure standalone services do not disrupt neighboring properties?

A: For urban renovation and waterproofing projects in tight Bangalore residential layouts, we implement strict site safety measures. This includes setting up high-density debris netting, scheduled low-noise working hours, and careful structural propping to guarantee zero disturbance or vibration damage to adjacent properties.

Q: How quickly can Neelachandra mobilize a team for a site assessment?

A: We can typically deploy a project manager or structural engineer to your site within 24 to 48 hours of receiving an inquiry. This applies to all locations within the greater Bangalore region for architectural consultations, renovation scoping, or urgent waterproofing inspections.
