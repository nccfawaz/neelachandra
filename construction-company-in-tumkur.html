Our Process

Our Construction Process: From Plot to Possession

Neelachandra follows a structured 5-stage process on every project in Tumkur. This keeps you informed at every milestone and ensures there are no surprises on cost or timeline.

1

Free Site Assessment & Feasibility Study

Our site engineer visits your Tumkur plot, assesses soil condition, checks TUDA/TMC setback norms, and evaluates access, drainage, and utility connection points. You receive a written feasibility summary within 3 working days.

2

Design, Floor Plan & Fixed-Cost Estimate

Our architects prepare floor plan options tailored to your site dimensions, family requirements, and budget. Once you approve the design, we issue a detailed cost estimate — itemised by work package — with no hidden escalation clauses.

3

Regulatory Approvals (TUDA / TMC Compliance)

Neelachandra manages the building plan approval process with TUDA or the Tumkur City Municipal Council on your behalf. Our experience with local planning regulations means approvals are obtained correctly the first time.

4

Construction & Milestone Reporting

Construction progresses through foundation, RCC framing, brickwork, roofing, plastering, and finishes. You receive photographic milestone reports at every key stage. Payments are tied to milestones, not arbitrary schedules.

5

Interior Finishing & Possession

Our interior team handles flooring, painting, kitchen, bathroom fixtures, and lighting — completing your home to handover-ready condition, with a joint walkthrough before closing out the contract.
