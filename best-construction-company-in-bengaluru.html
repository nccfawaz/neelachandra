Project Execution

Our Five-Stage Structural Execution Pipeline

Delays are prevented through predictable, accountable milestones. From our headquarters near Harsha Hospital on NH-4 to your site, our project management structure ensures absolute clarity from raw land analysis to final key delivery.

01
Legal Pre-Clearance

Site Diagnostics & Local Sanctions

We initiate thorough soil compaction testing and topography surveying. Concurrently, our internal liaison team manages layout checks and secures official plan sanctions and building permits.

02
Technical Pre-Engineering

Architectural Drafting & Fixed BOQ Calibration

Our architects draft complete 2D floor plans, structural wiring matrixes, and realistic 3D elevations. Once approved, an exact BOQ fixes your ultimate structural investment.

03
Hard Construction

Substructure & Superstructure Execution

Excavation, RCC footing layouts, plinth beam casting, and column raising proceed under continuous supervision by a dedicated site engineer. We conduct multi-point concrete slump tests at every critical slab level.

04
Systems Integration

Internal MEP & High-Performance Finishing

Concealed electrical grids, heavy-duty drainage lines, interior plastering, modular kitchen setups, and premium waterproofing are executed concurrently with the internal design architecture.

05
Quality Closeout

Structural Engineering Inspection

A definitive engineering check confirms compliance with all safety codes. We manage construction debris clearing, deep clean the property, and issue your keys alongside your 10-year structural warranty documents.

Regulatory Affairs

Comprehensive Liaison & Regulatory Management

Building a secure property requires deep alignment with regional municipal guidelines. Navigating zoning incorrectly risks massive fines or structural demolition. Neelachandra directly covers all paperwork across administrative divisions.

BBMP

Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike

Essential for structures inside core municipal city boundaries, ensuring accurate setback calculations and FAR allocations.

BMRDA & BDA

Metropolitan Region Development Authority

Critical for validating layout approvals, agricultural land conversions, and structural sanctions along expanding economic corridors.

GP

Gram Panchayat Sanctions

Handled expertly for properties situated outside municipal borders, with deep competence along industrial belts like Nelamangala and Dabaspet.
